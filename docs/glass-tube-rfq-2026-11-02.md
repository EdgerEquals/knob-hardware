## Watch: drawn-tube tolerances vs the model [C]
SCHOTT DURAN 42 × 2.3 is standard but OD ±0.50, wall ±0.11 (2.19–2.41). The case knob hole is the 42.0 OD + 0.2 radial, so a
42.5 tube would not fit; the caps are already printed to the measured tube. Ask for the OD tolerance, or ground OD (UQG), or
select/measure tubes and set the hole from the batch.
