## Power [Ed 09:40–09:45]
- USB-C PD at **up to 15 V, never 20 V**: the highest voltage reaches the board and is converted down there (the 2 Nov H7B0 plan said
  "ask for 9 V"; superseded). The PD sink asks for the highest offer up to 15 V.
- The **PD sink chip moves onto the little USB board** (was: on the main PCB). Only **15 V and GND** leave it.
- The two power wires are soldered direct (22 AWG, good for 3 A), not plugged. They drop to the jack board (below).
- [A] Main-board side: an input current limit (eFuse); the 5 V converter rated ≥ 30 V in; 35 V input capacitors; a TVS on VBUS on the
  USB board; a PD sink whose CC pins survive ≥ 20 V. [S] Apple's 20 W charger gives only 5 and 9 V; its 30 W gives 15 V
  (charger choice is still open: a 30 W-class charger, or the board also runs from 9 V).

## H7B0 off window D, onto the main board under the joystick [Ed 09:47–09:58]
- **H7B0 (LQFP100)** on the main-board **front** at about (0, 37), under the top board, between the joystick hold-down springs
  (2.9 clear). Window **D goes back to 917 mm²**.
- **Two vertical boards** stand on the main board at x ±16 (y 26.5–48.4, z 0–30, about 22 × 30 each, parts on both faces), with
  right-angle headers soldered at both edges. They sit behind the joystick's grip block, so they are not seen straight on.
  One standoff joins them at mid height. No standoffs down to the main board.
- The **top board** (joystick) solders to the vertical boards' top headers and **nests in a cradle in the case**. The case
  takes the joystick loads; presses go down through the vertical boards edge-on.
- **A and B stop at the grip block's edge (y 26.06)**: A 203 → 128, B 159 → 107 mm². The part removed was all behind the grip block.
- Checked: the H7B0 cannot sensibly sit on a vertical board (about 60 lines would cross a header; a 22 mm edge takes about 34 pins).

## Joystick cradle in the case [Ed 11:03]
- The joystick cradle's bottom part comes **under the faceplate**, with a **lip that fits the faceplate cutout**: the lip locates it
  sideways, the flange behind the faceplate takes the upward load, and it seals the opening.
- To get right [A]: a thin compressible gasket (0.5–1 mm) between flange and faceplate takes up the soldered stack's height
  tolerance and is the water seal; the lip shares the cutout with the moving carrier (±0.45 slide, 0.35 press) under the grip
  block's 0.9 overlap; lip clearance about 0.2 a side (SLA ±0.20, MJF ±0.25); a skirt or drain for water past the grip block.

## Jacks and USB-C lower [Ed 10:07, 11:09]
- **Jacks 5.45 mm lower** (centres y −39.7 → −45.15): the jack bodies' tops sit on the OLED's lit-area edge, so the whole lit area
  is seen (was 68%). The jacks stay inside the case.
- **USB-C 4.4 mm lower** (centre −49.3 → −53.65), its board 5.5 tall (was 6), in a **slot through the 2.9 mm bottom wall**, sealed
  with JB Weld. Its bottom edge is on the rail line (−56.4): that is the lowest it can go in a Eurorack case (the rail is behind the
  panel below it). A desktop case can give more room. USB board to jack bodies: 0.5 [C: raise the jacks 0.5 for more, 98% seen].
- [S] The Eurorack standard sets no maximum depth; "skiff-friendly" is about 25–40 mm. This module is about 53 mm behind the panel.

## Floorplan [Ed 11:22 "That all sounds good. Let's go with that"]
- **Vertical board L:** power: 15 V input limit, 15→5 V and 5→3.3 V converters (switchers on the outer face), filters and the
  MCU's analog supply on the inner face. Far from the Hall row and the jack circuits.
- **Under the knob, main-board back (noisy zone):** 1220 driver with current sense, motor-voice sensing and NTC; VCSEL decoder and
  switches, RGB, violet; the 716 driver (already there); the dial-screen link at the rod.
- **Main-board back, top:** NOR flash next to the chip; the **upper AMOLED's connector** (the upper AMOLED turns 180° so its flex
  leaves the top edge, near the chip).
- **Main-board back, bottom strip:** the **lower AMOLED's connector** (its flex rises from the screen's bottom edge). The strip takes
  only one connector.
- **Jack board (new):** a board under the four jacks (about z 25), **the jacks solder straight onto it** (PJ366ST are PCB jacks).
  Underside: CV out ×2, gate and audio out (op-amps and protection), CV and audio in (scaling, clamps, mono/stereo sense),
  ±12 V for CV, the clear OLED's 12.5 V supply ([S] the ER-OLED1.51-1 needs VCC 12–13.5 V) and its 24-pin ZIF (the 36 mm flex rises
  from the OLED's bottom edge). Two rows of long pins (2 × 2×6) drop into the main board's bottom strip at x ±9–16; the USB board's
  two wires land here. Hidden behind the jack bodies. Replaces the flying leads and the 14-way / 2-pin plugs.
- **Vertical board R:** CAN transceiver, the joystick lines up to the top board, an AMOLED supply if the flex lacks one [C],
  and spare room (Risley drivers later, I/O expander, test pads).
- **Top board:** SW1 and the press peg, switch ladder, the screen jumper socket, ESD and the 3.3 V filter.
- **Main board: 4 layers.** The tightest channel is the pair of ~2.3 mm strips beside D: about 26 lines must pass (lower AMOLED,
  jack-board link) against about 36–44 tracks on two signal layers. So each AMOLED runs on its own QSPI/SPI lines, not the
  shared 16-line 8080 bus [C: needs DisplayModule's tail pin list].
