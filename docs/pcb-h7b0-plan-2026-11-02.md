# Main PCB: STM32H7B0 signal plan and power budget (draft, 2 Nov 2026)

Tags: [S] source read, [A] estimate, [C] open check. Pin numbers are not assigned here: the FMC, Octo-SPI and FDCAN
alternate functions overlap on LQFP100, so the pin-out has to come from STM32CubeMX (or the datasheet pin table) [C].

## What the H7B0 has (ST product page [S]: https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers/stm32h7b0vb.html)
- Cortex-M7, 280 MHz, 128 KB flash, about 1.4 MB SRAM. LQFP100 lists **80 high-current I/Os**: that is the budget.
- 2× Octo-SPI, FMC (SRAM/PSRAM/NOR, so 8080-style LCD buses), 6× SPI, 4× I2C, FDCAN, USB OTG HS/FS, LCD-TFT controller.
- **No MIPI-DSI and no UCPD.** USB-C PD therefore needs a separate PD sink chip (the USB-C is power-only by decision).

## Changes from the PR #8 G4 map (62 + 19 signals)
- Dropped [Ed]: the scanner VCSEL (VCS_SCAN), the spinning mirror (MIRROR), the 2.0" TFT SPI group, UCPD CC1/CC2 and USB D+/D- (power-only USB-C).
- Added: two back AMOLEDs, the clear OLED, a quad NOR flash for animations, a PD sink chip.

## Power budget update [A]
PR #8 measured nothing yet; these are estimates on the +5 V rail.
- Two 2.4" AMOLEDs at 800 nits: about 1.8× the area and 1.8× the brightness of the 1.39" Kingtech (252 mW at full white, 450 nits [S]),
  so about **0.8 W each at full white** and roughly 0.15 W each on a dark UI.
- Peak (screens bright, both motors moving, show running): PR #8's 2.65 W minus the 2.0" TFT, plus about 1.6 W of AMOLED: **about 4.2 W**.
- Hardware ceiling (1220 at its stall clamp, everything else on): **about 5 W**, so about 1 A at 5 V.
- PD sink: ask for **9 V** (works with any 15 W or better charger), buck to 5 V on the main board. The 2-pin power plug (>= 3 A) has margin.
- Each AMOLED needs ELVDD/ELVSS from an AMOLED power chip on the main board unless the flex carries one [C].
