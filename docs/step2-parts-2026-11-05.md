Tight or open (analog):
- The switch is outside the op-amp loop: its resistance times the load current is a pitch error. TMUX7212 at 100 µA (10 V into 100 kΩ):
  0.2 mV ≈ 0.24 cent [A]; a 25-120 Ω switch would be 3-14 cents. So the low-Ω switch matters.
- Stability: 1 kΩ inside the loop + the switch's ~144 pF + cable puts a pole in the loop: split feedback (small cap output to -in, DC
  sensed after the resistor); bench-test with 1 nF on the jack [A].
- Negative headroom: the switches pass signals only between their rails (down to -12 V). A patched -12 V source sits at the edge;
  when the module is off (USB-powered, unplugged) and patched into a live case, about 10 mA per jack back-feeds through the clamps
  (±30 mA rating). Consider the board rail at -12.5 V for symmetric margin [A].
- DAC80504 stock is thin (103) and WQFN only: PCBWay to confirm early; fallback DAC8565.
- Ring sense: the ring can carry ±12 V from a stereo cable: 100 kΩ series into the TCA9535 pin. A mono plug shorts ring to sleeve:
  100 mA through the 33 Ω in MIDI mode (why the spec asks 0.5 W); firmware refuses MIDI on a jack that reads mono.

## Power and USB protection (power search, 5 Nov)

**HUSB238_002DD** (LCSC C7471904, 6,773 / from $0.288, DFN-10 3×3) [S]: _002DD = DFN-10, no SOP', OTP 150/130 °C, asks the "next
PDO" when the charger lacks 15 V. **VSET 14 kΩ = 15 V** (0 = 5, 6.04k = 9, 10k = 12, 17.8k = 18, open = 20 V); **ISET 22.6 kΩ = 3 A**
(open = 3.25 A default: don't leave open). Trap: the cdn.icstop.com PDF is the HUSB238**A** (different table: 11k = 15 V, 21k = 3 A).
Contract readable over I2C: PD_STATUS0 0x00 bits 7:4 voltage, 3:0 current; PD_STATUS1 0x01 CC_DIR, ATTACH, PD_RESPONSE, 5V_CURRENT
(Type-C level on a non-PD port); 0x02-0x07 the offered PDOs, 0x08 select, 0x09 GO. **I2C address 0x08** [S; 7-bit is the reading of
the Adafruit/ESP-IDF drivers, confirm by a bus scan]. D+/D- pins: only for Apple/BC1.2 detection; leaving them open is not stated
[A: looks safe, DNP pads to GND as an option; ask Hynetek]. Ratings: VIN/GATE 30 V, **CC 25 V (1.25× a 20 V fault: thinnest margin)**.

Tight or open (power):
- **A non-synchronous boost can't switch its output off**: with EN low it still passes VIN - Vd. "Off by default" for the ±12 V rails
  is done by the LDOs' EN pins (both take 3.3 V logic); the charge pump stops when the boost stops [S/A].
- TPS55340 at 15.75 V in: on-time ~105-125 ns vs its 77 ns minimum (1.4-1.6×) [A]; set VOUT about 18 V; above that it pulse-skips
  (harmless). Its maximum is 1.2 MHz.
- Charge-pump diode peaks up to the inductor peak (~0.8 A) [A] vs BAT54SFILM 1.25 A surge: series 1-2.2 Ω or the PMEG4010CEJ pair.
- Thinnest margins on VBUS: HUSB238 CC 25 V (1.25×); the TVS clamp at 10 A (29-31 V) reaches the HUSB238's 30 V VIN and the
  TPS62933's 32 V: fine for ESD and hot-plug ringing, not for big surges [A].
- To confirm: HUSB238 D+/D- left open, its 7-bit 0x08, the TPS62933 EN when floating.
