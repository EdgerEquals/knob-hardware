Tight or open (analog):
- The switch is outside the op-amp loop: its resistance times the load current is a pitch error. TMUX7212 at 100 µA (10 V into 100 kΩ):
  0.2 mV ≈ 0.24 cent [A]; a 25-120 Ω switch would be 3-14 cents. So the low-Ω switch matters.
- Stability: 1 kΩ inside the loop + the switch's ~144 pF + cable puts a pole in the loop: split feedback (small cap output to -in, DC
  sensed after the resistor); bench-test with 1 nF on the jack [A].
- Negative headroom: the switches pass signals only between their rails (down to -12 V). A patched -12 V source sits at the edge;
  when the module is off (USB-powered, unplugged) and patched into a live case, about 10 mA per jack back-feeds through the clamps
  (±30 mA rating). Consider the board rail at -12.5 V for symmetric margin [A].
- DAC80504 stock is thin (103) and WQFN only: PCBWay to confirm early; fallback DAC8565.
- Ring sense: the ring can carry ±12 V from a stereo cable: 100 kΩ series into the TCA9535 pin. A mono plug shorts ring to sleeve:
  100 mA through the 33 Ω in MIDI mode (why the spec asks 0.5 W); firmware refuses MIDI on a jack that reads mono.
