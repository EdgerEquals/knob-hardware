## What changes in the model and the board
- The back AMOLED outline goes from 38.68 to **38.72** wide (0.04 mm; the model value came from an earlier listing).
- The clear OLED thickness is **1.25** [S].
- The H7B0 has no MIPI-DSI. Drive the two back AMOLEDs over the **FMC 8080 bus** (8-bit, one chip select each), or over SPI if the frame rate is enough. QSPI/OctoSPI only if the panel's IM straps allow it [C].
- The back AMOLEDs need an **AMOLED power chip** (ELVDD/ELVSS) on the main board unless the flex carries one [C: confirm from the drawing].
- The clear OLED needs a small **boost (~12 V)** for its panel drive if the bare panel is used [C].

## Back AMOLED: DisplayModule DM-AMOLED240-011 datasheet read (2 Nov, 15:00) [S]
Danny supplied the datasheet (`docs/datasheets/DM-AMOLED240-011_Datasheet.pdf`, drawing A3 2022-12-07). It is the same panel as the model:
RM690B0, 450×600, outline 38.72 × 51.56, active 36.72 × 48.96, 0.78 thick (0.90 with the protective film).
- **Flex exits the bottom short edge** (the 38.72 side), roughly centred (its tail end starts 12.46 from the left edge), and is **50.0 long**
  from the glass edge, unfolded. It is drawn folding back **behind the panel**, inside its outline, with up to **1.0** of tape and parts behind it.
- The flex carries a component area: the block diagram shows a **DC-DC on the flex** making ELVDD/ELVSS/VCI/VDDIO from the host supply,
  so the main board may not need an AMOLED power chip [C: confirm the tail pin list].
- **Tail end: 22 gold fingers at 0.6 mm pitch** (12.60 span) on a 0.20 PI stiffener. The revision note calls it a **FOF (flex-on-flex)
  bonding end**, i.e. hot-bar bonded, not a standard ZIF [C: ask DisplayModule for the tail pin list and whether a 0.5/0.6 ZIF version exists].
- Interfaces: SPI / 8080 MCU / MIPI (QSPI not listed; Waveshare runs the same RM690B0 over QSPI on its 2.41" board) [C].
- Supplies: VCI 2.7–3.6 (3.3), VDDIO 1.65–3.3 (1.8), ELVDD 2.0–6 (3.6), ELVSS −4.7 to −0.4 (−3.6), VCI_EN logic enable.

## Joystick screen: what the Waveshare 1.64" board files show (2 Nov, 15:05) [S]
Danny supplied the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64 structure zip (outline PDF, DWG, STEP) and schematic
(`docs/datasheets/Waveshare-ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-*.pdf`). The STEP names the panel **MS-DO0164FMST02**, the sister of our FMST05.
- Cover glass **26.74 × 41.62**, module **1.5** thick (same as our DO0164FMST05 numbers); visible area 22.34 × 36.06.
- **The flex does not hang off as a tail.** It leaves a short edge (bends ~1.0 inside the glass edge, about 12 wide plus a ~3 wide
  side strip), **folds back under the panel**, and stays inside the outline (x ±11.4). Parts on it reach 1.2 behind the panel.
- **Connector behind the panel:** a 30-pin 0.4 mm board-to-board header (8.0 × 2.5 plug, 8.6 × 4.0 base) on the folded flex,
  centred across the width, **~11.7 from the panel centre toward the end away from the flex exit**, mating face **2.15 behind** the panel
  back, facing away from the glass. Picture: `docs/img/DO0164FMST02-back-from-waveshare-step.png` (from the back).
- Pin-out on the Waveshare board (receptacle side, 30 pins + 4 shell): 1/2/16/22/28 GND, 3 TP_SCL, 5 TP_SDA, 7 TP_INT (nc), 9 TP_RESET (nc),
  11 TP_VCC 3V3, 13 VPP (nc), 15 IM1 = 3V3, 17 IM0 = GND (QSPI), 19 RESET, 21 PWR_EN (10k to 3V3), 23 VCI 3V3, 25 VDDIO 3V3, 27 TE (nc on
  Waveshare; we want it), 29/30 VBAT 3V3; 4 CLK, 6 CS, 8/10/12/14 D3/D2/D1/D0; 18/20/24/26 MIPI pairs tied to GND. One 3.3 V rail.
- **Consequence for the joystick model [C, decision for Danny]:** v0.5 assumed a tail leaving +x, folding down through the roof and
  carrier floor to an AXE530127 on a daughterboard tab. With the FMST02 flex the connector sits 2.15 behind the glass, inside the grip
  block: the roof needs a pocket for the folded flex and the plug, and a short jumper (flex with the mating receptacle) has to run from
  there down to the daughterboard with slack for the slide and press. Whether FMST05 differs is not known; the Waveshare test board
  carries FMST02.

## Datasheets Danny supplied, 2 Nov 15:08 [S] (all in `docs/datasheets/`)
- **CO5300** (joystick screen and the 1.32" dial candidate): QSPI when IM[1:0] = 10; QSPI write clock cycle ≥ 20 ns (**50 MHz**),
  read ≥ 100 ns; VDDI 1.65–3.3, VCI 2.7–3.6. In COF form VCC ties to VDDI on the flex.
- **RM690B0** (back screens): **QSPI is supported** (IM[1:0] = 10: MIPI / QUAD-SPI; 00 3-wire SPI, 01 4-wire SPI, 11 MCU 8-bit).
  QSPI write cycle ≥ 20 ns (**50 MHz**), read ≥ 150 ns. So the H7B0 can drive both back screens over (Octo/Q)SPI, not the 8080 bus,
  **if** the DM-AMOLED240-011 flex brings IM0/IM1 and D0–D3 out (its 22-finger tail pin list is still unknown) [C].
- **ER-OLED1.51-1** (clear OLED, EastRising): 42.04 × 27.22 × 1.25, active 35.05 × 15.32, **128 × 56** dots (not 128 × 64), SSD1309.
  Flex leaves the middle of a **long (42.04) edge**, **36.0 ± 0.5** long from the panel edge, 24 contacts at 0.5 mm (11.5 span, 12.5 wide)
  on a 4.5 × 0.3 stiffener; it can fold back under the panel (2.05 total when folded). VDD 1.65–3.3 (2.8 typ);
  **VCC 12–13.5 V (12.5 typ) supplied externally**: about 15 mA at half the pixels on, 34 mA max. So the main board needs a ~12.5 V boost.
- **ER-CON24HT-1**: the matching 24-pin 0.5 mm ZIF, SMD horizontal, top contact; 17.65 × 4.5 × 2.0.
